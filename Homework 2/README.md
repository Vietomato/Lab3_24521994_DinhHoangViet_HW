
## Built-in Beethoven piano playback

Open the app and press **Play Beethoven Virus**. Audio initializes from that
click; no recording or download is required at runtime. Stop cancels playback,
and Replay speed selects 0.5x, 1x or 2x for the next run. Saved recordings are
preserved and cannot overlap playback.

The bundled score is Beethoven's Pathétique Sonata, Op. 13, movement III,
using the public-domain Chris Sawer/Mutopia edition. It plays the original piano
movement (2,385 notes, about 3m53s at 1x), not an exact commercial remix.
Source/license and regeneration instructions are in assets/README.md.
Dense passages follow the existing eight-voice FIFO limit. Audible musical
quality still needs human listening; automated tests verify scheduling/UI only.
